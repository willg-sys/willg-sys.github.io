Ultrasonic Rangefinder

PCB Design Power Regulation

A battery-powered rangefinder board, and the project that taught me PCB production end to end. Two AA cells feed boost converters stepping up to 3.3 V for the MCU and 5 V for the sensor; the regulators are WEBENCH-simulated at 90.1% / 91.2% efficiency and measured at roughly 93% on the built board under a 100 Ω load.

Systems Tester Board

Test & Validation PCB Design

An end-of-line PCBA tester that automates firmware flashing, measurement, and pass/fail validation, pairing custom hardware with host-side software for accurate, repeatable results. The 4-layer board follows DFM principles, with routing, ground planes, and thermal reliefs tuned for manufacturability and long-term reliability, and coordinated directly with the fab house. An analog front end (dividers, current monitors, regulators) was validated in LTspice for stability across load conditions, and an STM32 controller handles on-chip ADC acquisition, error detection, and serial reporting back to the host PC.

Audio Amplifier (2023)

Analog Design PCB Design

A dual-channel, 2 W audio amplifier built around the TI LM386, with the gain-setting network and decoupling tuned to cut distortion and quiet the power rail. Verified in LTspice, laid out as a 2-layer board in Altium with careful grounding and trace separation for EMI, and fabricated through PCBWay. Bench-validated on a 5 V wall adapter and a salvaged speaker — the original schematics didn't survive a dead laptop, so what's shown is a quick reconstruction.
